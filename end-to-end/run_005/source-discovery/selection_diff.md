<span style="background:#fff4e5;color:#b54708;padding:0.08em 0.18em;">CREDIT HISTORY AND CREDIT SCORE SUMMARY (SCORE) INFORMATION</span>

<span style="background:#fff4e5;color:#b54708;padding:0.08em 0.18em;">This document presents the information required by RA legislation regarding credit history and credit score summary (score), the factors affecting it, and their use in the lending process by Ameriabank CJSC (hereinafter referred to as the &quot;Bank&quot;).</span>

<span style="background:#fff4e5;color:#b54708;padding:0.08em 0.18em;">Credit history is the information created/processed by a credit bureau regarding a customer&#x27;s financial obligations, reflecting the customer&#x27;s debts, payments, payment habits, and the dynamics/history of this information.</span>

<span style="background:#fff4e5;color:#b54708;padding:0.08em 0.18em;">Credit history includes, in particular, the identification data of the customer, the amount of each obligation, annual interest rate, outstanding balance, payment frequency and due dates, delays in payments, guarantees provided for third parties, depersonalized information about obligations of related parties, as well as data on inquiries made about the credit history.</span>

<span style="background:#fff4e5;color:#b54708;padding:0.08em 0.18em;">As a rule, credit history is used by financial institutions to assess a customer&#x27;s ability to assume/maintain financial obligations, act as a guarantor, evaluate lending opportunities either at the initiative of the customer or the Bank, or provide loan offers to the customer.</span>

<span style="background:#fff4e5;color:#b54708;padding:0.08em 0.18em;">Based on the customer&#x27;s consent, the Bank submits an inquiry to ACRA Credit Reporting. Credit history information is reflected in a credit report that contains the customer&#x27;s credit history for the previous 5 years as of the date of inquiry.</span>

<span style="background:#fff4e5;color:#b54708;padding:0.08em 0.18em;">The customer can review their credit history at http://www.acra.am/.</span>

<span style="background:#fff4e5;color:#b54708;padding:0.08em 0.18em;">The credit score is a numerical evaluation of the customer&#x27;s creditworthiness and reliability.</span>

<span style="background:#fff4e5;color:#b54708;padding:0.08em 0.18em;">The Bank uses its own creditworthiness and reliability evaluation system for lending purposes, with the main influencing factors listed below:</span>

- <span style="background:#fff4e5;color:#b54708;padding:0.08em 0.18em;">Credit history: The number and amount of active loans, frequency of loan applications, and a positive credit history (no delays, high-quality servicing) can help improve the score and lead to loan approval. A poor credit history (delays, long-term delinquencies) may lower the score and result in rejection or stricter conditions.</span>

- <span style="background:#fff4e5;color:#b54708;padding:0.08em 0.18em;">Employment, income, and work experience: Having stable employment/income can increase the score and lead to approval. Unstable or irregular income/employment may lower the score and lead to rejection or stricter terms.</span>

- <span style="background:#fff4e5;color:#b54708;padding:0.08em 0.18em;">Credit burden: A high debt-to-income ratio can negatively impact the score, while a low credit burden can help improve the score and lead to approval.</span>

- <span style="background:#fff4e5;color:#b54708;padding:0.08em 0.18em;">FICO Score: A FICO score is a statistical scoring model based on credit history used globally to assess credit risk. Details at acra.am.</span>

<span style="background:#fff4e5;color:#b54708;padding:0.08em 0.18em;">The importance of credit history and score</span>

<span style="background:#fff4e5;color:#b54708;padding:0.08em 0.18em;">Credit history and credit score play a critical role in the decision-making process for loan approval, allowing banks and credit organizations to assess the level of credit risk and predict the customer’s likelihood of proper repayment.</span>

<span style="background:#fff4e5;color:#b54708;padding:0.08em 0.18em;">Incorrect or incomplete credit history</span>

<span style="background:#fff4e5;color:#b54708;padding:0.08em 0.18em;">If the credit history contains errors or incomplete data, the customer may apply to ACRA Credit Reporting CJSC (credit bureau) or the financial organization that provided the incorrect data.</span>

<span style="background:#fff4e5;color:#b54708;padding:0.08em 0.18em;">For inquiries to ACRA Credit Reporting CJSC, the procedure and detailed description are available at acra.am.</span>

<span style="background:#fff4e5;color:#b54708;padding:0.08em 0.18em;">If the incorrect or incomplete data was transferred by the Bank, the customer may submit a request via any of the following methods:</span>

<span style="background:#fff4e5;color:#b54708;padding:0.08em 0.18em;">The Bank processes the customer&#x27;s request in accordance with the RA legislation and/or internal legal regulations of the Bank, within the defined timeframes.</span>

<span style="background:#fff4e5;color:#b54708;padding:0.08em 0.18em;">Steps to improve credit history and credit score</span>

<span style="background:#fff4e5;color:#b54708;padding:0.08em 0.18em;">To improve credit history and score, it is necessary to eliminate the root causes as soon as possible, particularly:</span>

- <span style="background:#fff4e5;color:#b54708;padding:0.08em 0.18em;">Make regular loan payments according to the schedule, avoiding even a single day’s delay</span>

- <span style="background:#fff4e5;color:#b54708;padding:0.08em 0.18em;">Reduce the number and amount of guarantees provided; if possible, ensure repayment of overdue guaranteed obligations</span>

- <span style="background:#fff4e5;color:#b54708;padding:0.08em 0.18em;">Ease the credit burden by paying off overdue obligations</span>

- <span style="background:#fff4e5;color:#b54708;padding:0.08em 0.18em;">Avoid frequent applications for new loans, as credit history inquiries (except those for loan monitoring) may negatively impact the score</span>
